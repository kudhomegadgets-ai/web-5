FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend visiting the dentist every six months for regular check-ups and professional cleanings. These routine visits help prevent dental problems and catch any issues early when they are easier and less expensive to treat.
We accept most major dental insurance plans including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, United Healthcare, and Guardian. Contact our office to verify your specific plan and coverage before your appointment.
Call our emergency line immediately at (123) 456-7890. We offer same-day emergency appointments for urgent dental situations such as severe tooth pain, knocked-out teeth, broken teeth, or dental abscesses. We are here to help when you need us most.
Dental implants are an excellent solution for most adults with good overall health and adequate jaw bone density. During a consultation, our team will evaluate your oral health, discuss your options, and determine if implants are the best choice for you.
We understand dental anxiety is very common. Our team is trained to provide gentle, compassionate care. We offer sedation options, take time to explain every step, and create a calm, welcoming environment to help you feel comfortable throughout your visit.
